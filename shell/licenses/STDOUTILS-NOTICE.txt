The copy this installer carries
--------------------------------

The `StdUtils.dll` in this installer is a verbatim copy of the upstream
StdUtils 1.14 release build of 2018-10-27: it is byte for byte the file
`Plugins/Unicode/StdUtils.dll` from the upstream release archive
`StdUtils.2018-10-27.zip`. It is not modified, and no part of the
StdUtils plug-in Library's source code is incorporated into any other part of
the LabCairn distribution.

The installer calls the plug-in strictly through the NSIS plug-in interface,
and the copy it carries is the whole of the Library as distributed here. The
author's own clarification of what that means for a distributor is
reproduced verbatim in `STDOUTILS-LGPL-CLARIFICATION.txt`, which travels with
this document; under it, an installer that incorporates and uses the plug-in
through the plug-in interface and distributes verbatim copies of it is a "work
that uses the Library" rather than a derivative of it, and may be distributed
under the terms of the distributor's choice.

Replaceability
--------------

The plug-in may be replaced. Nothing in this distribution restricts its
modification, and LGPL-2.1 §2 permits a modified version to be distributed
under the terms of §1, provided that the modified work carries the notices §2
requires and is itself accompanied by its corresponding source code.

In this distribution the plug-in is a build-time component of the installer
rather than a component of the installed application: the installer extracts
it to `$PLUGINSDIR` at install time and calls it there, and it is not
installed as a file of its own. Replacing it therefore means replacing the
copy inside the installer archive — a build of the plug-in that keeps the
`StdUtils::TestParameter` export and its calling convention can be put in the
archive's place before the installer runs.
