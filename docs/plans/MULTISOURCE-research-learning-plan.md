## Purpose and integration

Extend the paper-revision trajectory into a research trajectory. A LabCairn project can record how a research question was raised, what alternatives were debated, why one option was chosen, what was tried, what the results supported, how the result was presented and what changed during manuscript review. A new paper can then retrieve a relevant, reviewed case and explain where its lesson applies. The same case can help prepare a meeting, review an experimental plan or revise a slide deck.

Build on the paper trajectory, feedback chain, evidence anchors and guidance-rule lifecycle. The existing [distillation](../../src/labcairn/guidance/distil.py) and [retrieval](../../src/labcairn/guidance/retrieval.py) paths resolve comments and revision events. Add typed research events and source resolution behind a new ADR. Preserve the proof that a paper comment was addressed, and require a reviewed outcome before treating a meeting or slide as evidence that a research decision worked. Version provenance and evidence anchors stay in the existing project boundary. The original paper-only route remains a regression baseline.

```text
Source version → verifiable anchor → observed statement or change
               → proposed link → reviewed research trajectory
               → scoped case or guidance rule → retrieval with evidence
```

## Source contracts

| Source | Anchor and version | What to extract | Review boundary |
|---|---|---|---|

Separate a discussion point from a decision and a decision from an observed outcome. Unclear segments remain unconfirmed. |
| Group presentation PPT/PPTX | File fingerprint, revision, slide index and object or note reference where available | Slide argument, table or figure, speaker notes, reported progress, open issues and evidence links | Distinguish plans, preliminary figures and reviewed results. Keep notes and embedded media under their own access rules. Label OCR and attach the slide crop or location for verification. |
| Manuscripts in progress, submissions and revision rounds | Paper project, immutable version, paragraph/comment/revision locator, round and responsible party | Draft claim, proposed edit, advisor or reviewer rationale, author response and eventual outcome | Reuse DOCX feedback-chain semantics. Coauthor drafts, reviewer files and journal correspondence are separate assets with their own scope. A model-suggested relationship stays a proposal until reviewed. |
| Published papers and supplements | DOI or publication identifier when available, publication/version status, page, section, figure or table | Published methods, results, conclusion, stated limits and supporting evidence | Verify the publication version and applicable access and reuse terms. The current [locator vocabulary](../../src/labcairn/sources/locators.py) already covers PDF and DOCX. The ADR must define new audio-interval and slide/object anchors, including parser/transcript versioning, stable identity and how a new source version invalidates older references. The first batch can use manually curated anchors to test the contract before adding bulk adapters.

Register standalone decks and papers even when no meeting or revision link exists. They can serve as a scoped project reference or a presentation example after review. A paper from a separate research project enters that project's scope unless an explicit sharing grant permits reuse. Case records must be able to represent failed experiments, discarded ideas and unresolved disagreements alongside successful work. Missing links can be added later through the same reviewed promotion path.

## Research trajectory and learning workflow

1. **Register.** Record project, source version and hash, material owner or custodian, sensitivity, permitted purposes, retention and any participant or coauthor restrictions. Record duplicate source hashes and keep distinct versions. Assign a pseudonymous speaker ID only within its verified scope. Keep identity mappings apart from searchable text.
2. **Process.** Prefer local transcription. Store the original separately from versioned raw and corrected transcripts. Preserve timing, overlap, uncertainty and the changes made during correction. Extract PPT structure, speaker notes and chart references separately. Use existing manuscript/PDF ingestion where supported. A cloud transcriber or external model needs its own approved outbound scope.
3. **Link.** Record observed statements as events with project, time, actor type, source anchor, event type and provenance. Use separate link types for `raises`, `proposes`, `objects`, `decides`, `tests`, `reports`, `revises`, `supports`, `contradicts` and `supersedes`. Cross-file matches start as candidates unless an explicit reference establishes the link. Record evidence and reviewer identity for any promotion. Unmatched files and missing outcomes stay visible. A later contradictory result can supersede a working assumption without erasing it.
4. **Review.** Verify the proposed source join, speaker attribution, chronology, interpretation and outcome separately. A case may remain incomplete and searchable as a project record under read permissions. A guidance candidate needs an explicit use scope, a traceable source chain, an observed outcome or documented unresolved status, counterexamples and applicability limits. Only a human-reviewed, outcome-supported version can be served as a reusable recommendation. An unresolved case is labelled as context.
5. **Publish and retrieve.** Reuse the versioned guidance-rule lifecycle. Each version names its supporting evidence, grant references, project/subject scope, author and reviewer, known limits and verification status. Recheck effective access at query time, including cross-project requests and any outbound request. Show the user the source passages and why they apply. A new result triggers review of affected rules, with versioned correction or retirement.

**Illustrative case.** A meeting raises the concern that a single membrane-cleaning metric may support a narrower conclusion than the team intends. A later deck presents two metrics, a draft limits its claim, an advisor comment asks for a particular comparison, and the final paper reports the comparison. LabCairn links these as separate evidence-backed events. The reviewer confirms whether the data and revisions addressed the concern, then publishes a bounded lesson or leaves it unresolved. The same lesson can later surface during a draft or deck review with the timestamp, slide and manuscript locations the reader is authorised to see.

## Permissions and withdrawal

- Keep intake/read, parsing, project retrieval, persistent learning, group sharing and external transmission as distinct decisions. ADR-0015 already provides `store_read`, `parse_annotate`, `external_transmit` and `train_reuse`, separately from operation approval. Reuse their checks. The new ADR must settle how project-scoped reusable retrieval and group publication fit the current grant model, with a named audience and purpose. Until that contract exists, the pilot demonstrates scope and withdrawal using a local manifest and synthetic or expressly authorised material.
- A source appearing in an archive, shared folder or publication is a registration opportunity. Import requires a recorded permitted use. An audio batch uses only segments whose relevant permissions are confirmed. Material with unresolved participant, coauthor, publisher or confidentiality conditions stays pending outside the search and learning indexes. No retrospective assumption of permission based on who submitted the file.
- Keep original audio and other raw sources in the authorised local or private environment. Use reviewed excerpts and pseudonyms in approved outputs. Consent to transcription, summarisation, long-term reuse or wider sharing must be visible separately. Cross-project sharing requires an explicit permission and review. The first release uses retrieval and reviewed case or rule publication. Model fine-tuning would require a separate decision and evidence plan.
- Each transcript segment, extracted slide, candidate link, index or embedding entry and published rule records its source version and effective grants. On withdrawal, stop use immediately, cancel queued work, invalidate search, caches and derived copies, and re-evaluate any rule with multiple supporting sources. Suspend it if remaining evidence cannot support the same wording. Reconcile invalidations after restart and apply them during backup restore. Retain an audit record with minimal metadata. Handle previously exported or externally transmitted copies under the recorded disposition for that transfer.

### Evaluation protocol

Separate three questions in the report. First, measure extraction fidelity per modality, including audio time and speaker attribution, slide and paper locators, and high-risk terminology or numerical errors. Second, measure trajectory-link correctness and whether each rule is supported by its cited outcome. Third, compare the same unseen review task with the same model and budget using the paper-only baseline and the expanded memory. Log the input corpus, version, permissions, latency and human effort. Choose sample sizes and decision thresholds before a comparative run. A single pilot case establishes a workflow demonstration and yields qualitative feedback. It cannot establish a general capability gain.

- The initial case custodian identifies the materials, participants with authority to review them, allowed excerpts and locally retained copies. Confirm retention duration, backup treatment and the person responsible for withdrawal before increasing volume.

The wider Q81 policy for every imported research file remains tracked in the [design traceability record](PHASE_3-design-traceability.md).
- Larger batches, external transcription, model training, publication outside the research group and cross-project sharing each have their own decision points. Record the tool or recipient, purpose, content, retention and review rules before scheduling that work.
